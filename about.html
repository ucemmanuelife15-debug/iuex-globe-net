IUEX Globe.Net is a technology company dedicated to building innovative digital products and services that empower individuals, businesses, and communities through software, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, AI, and digital transformation.

Our Story

IUEX Globe.Net was founded with a simple but ambitious belief: that technology, when built thoughtfully, can open doors — connecting people to skills, opportunities, and each other. What started as a vision to build practical, real-world digital products has grown into a company developing multiple products, including ELink and ERoute, alongside coding education designed to equip the next generation of African tech talent.
